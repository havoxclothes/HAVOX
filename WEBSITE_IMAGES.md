## 3. HOW TO CHANGE THEM

1. Upload your chosen images into the repository's `assets` folder.
2. Copy the exact filename, including spaces and `.png` / `.jpg`.
3. Replace the filename after `assets/` above.
4. Commit the changes.
5. Tell ChatGPT **"I updated WEBSITE_IMAGES.md"** and the website can be updated to use your choices.

**Important:** Do not rename or delete the image files unless you also update the filenames in this file.
